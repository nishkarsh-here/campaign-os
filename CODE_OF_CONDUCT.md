# Code of Conduct

Campaign OS aims to be a welcoming, respectful project for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Examples of behavior that supports this aim include:

- Being considerate and constructive in discussion and review.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and receiving feedback with care.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Respecting privacy and avoiding publication of personal or confidential
  information.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and sharing private
information without consent are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the
project safe and productive.

Report a conduct concern privately through GitHub's contact options for the
repository owner. Please include relevant links and any context needed to
understand the concern. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and fairly.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the
project in public spaces.
